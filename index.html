Services

Everything your storefront
needs to sell better.

One senior team across design, Liquid and apps — so strategy, build and optimisation never get lost in a handover.

01

Shopify Theme Development & Customization

Bespoke themes, modular sections and code standards using Liquid and JSON templates — built to scale with your catalogue and stay editable by your marketing team.

02

UI/UX Design & CRO

Conversion-focused design, heuristic reviews, A/B testing plans and friction-free checkout flows that move add-to-cart and completed orders, not just aesthetics.

03

Performance Optimization

Core Web Vitals work on LCP, CLS and INP, plus Lighthouse tuning, asset governance and third-party auditing — because paid traffic is wasted on a storefront that loads slowly on mobile.

04

Seamless Migrations to Shopify

Replatforming from WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce or Salesforce Commerce Cloud with clean data migration, redirect mapping, SEO preserved through launch, and ERP, fulfilment and payment integrations rebuilt on the other side.

05

Custom Shopify App Development

Private and public apps on Node.js and Remix with the GraphQL Admin API — Shopify Functions for discounts and delivery, Cart Transform, Theme App Extensions, webhooks and App Proxy to remove manual work from pricing, fulfilment and merchandising.

06

Product Pages & Premium Landing Pages

Fully customised PDPs and PLPs plus high-end campaign landers engineered for engagement, average order value and conversion.

07

Shopify Plus, B2B & Checkout

Wholesale catalogues, company accounts, Markets and multi-currency, and custom checkout built on Checkout Extensibility — the path forward now that Scripts and checkout.liquid are behind us.

08

Agency Consultancy & White-Label Delivery

We work behind the scenes as the Shopify specialists for agencies in Sweden and beyond — technical discovery, architecture reviews and extra build capacity, delivered under your brand and to your deadlines.
